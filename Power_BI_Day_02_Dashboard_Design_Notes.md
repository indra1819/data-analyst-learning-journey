## 1. Dashboard Page Setup

Created a professional dashboard canvas using a widescreen layout.

### Recommended Page Size

- Aspect Ratio: 16:9
- Suitable for desktop dashboard presentation
- Provides enough space for KPIs and charts

---

## 2. Dashboard Background

Used a clean background to improve readability.

### Best Practices

- Use a light and simple background
- Avoid distracting images
- Maintain consistent colors
- Keep enough space between visuals

---

## 3. Header Section

Created a dedicated header area at the top of the dashboard.

### Header Contains

- Dashboard title
- Report name
- Professional visual structure

Example:

> Banking Analytics Dashboard

---

## 4. Shapes

Used Power BI shapes to create dashboard containers and sections.

### Shapes Used For

- Header background
- KPI card backgrounds
- Section containers
- Visual grouping
- Professional dashboard structure

Shapes help separate different sections of the report.

---

## 5. KPI Card Layout

Designed the top section for important business KPIs.

Example:

- Total Sales
- Total Cost
- Total Profit
- Total Orders

### KPI Design Principles

- Keep KPIs at the top
- Use consistent card sizes
- Keep labels short
- Highlight important numbers
- Maintain equal spacing

---

## 6. Chart Placement

Charts should be arranged according to business importance.

### Recommended Layout

Top:
- KPI Cards

Middle:
- Main business trend
- Category analysis

Bottom:
- Product analysis
- Additional business insights

---

## 7. Visual Alignment

Professional dashboards require consistent alignment.

### Important Points

- Keep charts aligned
- Use equal spacing
- Maintain consistent widths
- Avoid overlapping visuals
- Keep margins consistent

---

## 8. Dashboard Visual Hierarchy

The dashboard should guide the user's attention.

### Recommended Hierarchy

1. Dashboard Title
2. KPI Cards
3. Main Business Trend
4. Category / Product Analysis
5. Detailed Analysis
6. Business Insights

---

## 9. Professional Dashboard Principles

A good dashboard should be:

- Simple
- Clean
- Consistent
- Easy to understand
- Business-focused
- Visually balanced

Avoid:

- Too many colors
- Too many charts
- Unnecessary decorations
- Crowded layouts
- Difficult-to-read text

---

## 10. Data Analyst Perspective

Dashboard design is not only about making visuals attractive.

The main objective is to answer business questions quickly.

Examples:

- What are total sales?
- Which category performs best?
- Which products generate the most revenue?
- How are sales changing over time?
- Where is the business performing poorly?

---
